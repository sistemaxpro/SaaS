## Prompt maestro
Create a vertical 9:16 commercial video for a SaaS inventory management system called SistemaX. Duration around 45 seconds. Style: premium corporate, modern technology, clean and polished, realistic business environment, cinematic commercial quality, smooth transitions, professional blue and white color palette.

Scene 1: show a small retail business with inventory disorder, stacked boxes, missing products, and a worried employee checking shelves.
Scene 2: show a premium inventory SaaS dashboard on laptop and tablet, clean modern UI, real-time indicators, clear and elegant design.
Scene 3: show an employee scanning products and registering stock entries and exits with a barcode scanner and tablet.
Scene 4: show alerts, reports, charts, and stock notifications inside the interface.
Scene 5: show the business now organized and efficient, employee working confidently, shelves in order, smooth operations.
Scene 6: final premium brand reveal with SistemaX logo over a blurred dashboard background and strong CTA.
